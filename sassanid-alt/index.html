Derafsh Kaviani (alternate)

Description

  • An alternative representation of the Sassanid Empire's banner, typically featuring the Derafsh Kaviani — a legendary royal standard with streamers and precious stones
  • The Sassanid Empire (224–651 CE) was the last pre-Islamic Persian empire
  • Their royal banners were elaborate, jewelled standards rather than simple cloth flags
  • Construction: varies; the Derafsh Kaviani was a leather apron mounted on a lance, adorned with jewels. No standard ratio

Trivia

  • Alternative Sassanid flag design with different symbolism
  • Represents variations in royal banners and regalia
  • Shows the evolution of Sassanid heraldic traditions

Derafsh Kaviani (alternate)

An alternative representation of the Sassanid Empire's banner, typically featuring the Derafsh Kaviani — a legendary royal standard with streamers and precious stones. The Sassanid Empire (224–651 CE) was the last pre-Islamic Persian empire.

Alternative Sassanid flag design with different symbolism. Represents variations in royal banners and regalia. Shows the evolution of Sassanid heraldic traditions.
