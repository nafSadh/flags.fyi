# Flag of Bermuda

A Red Ensign with the coat of arms of Bermuda in the fly. The arms depict a red lion holding a shield showing a sinking ship, representing the wreck of the Sea Venture in 1609.

The lion on the shield holds a key, symbolising Bermuda's strategic location in Atlantic trade routes. Bermuda's flag features the Union Jack, reflecting its status as a British Overseas Territory. The red is said to reference the red earth found on Bermuda's beaches.
