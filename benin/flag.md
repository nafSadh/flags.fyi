# Flag of Benin

Benin's flag consists of a green vertical band on the hoist side and two horizontal bands of yellow over red on the fly. Originally adopted on 16 November 1959 as French Dahomey prepared for independence, it uses the Pan-African colour palette: green for hope and the southern palm groves, yellow for the northern savannas and national wealth, and red for the courage of the ancestors.

The flag was replaced in 1975 by a green field with a red star when the country became the Marxist-Leninist People's Republic of Benin. After the collapse of that regime, the original tricolour was restored on 1 August 1990, marking the return to pluralist democracy and reconnecting the country with its independence-era identity.
