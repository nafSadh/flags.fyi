# Flag of Kuwait

A horizontal triband of green, white, and red with a black trapezoid on the hoist side. Adopted on 7 September 1961, shortly after independence from Britain, and officially hoisted on 24 November 1961. The colours are inspired by a poem by Safie Al-Deen Al-Hili.

The black trapezoid represents the enemy; the green represents Islamic faith; red represents bloodshed. The white represents purity and peaceful intentions. Kuwait adopted this flag in 1961 upon independence from Britain.
