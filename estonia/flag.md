# Flag of Estonia

Estonia's flag is a horizontal tricolour of blue, black, and white in three equal bands. It was first created by students at the University of Tartu in 1881 as the banner of the Estonian Students' Society and consecrated at Otepää parish church on 4 June 1884 — a date now honoured as Estonian Flag Day. After independence the tricolour was adopted nationally in 1922.

Banned during the Soviet occupation, the flag was re-adopted on 7 August 1990 as Estonia regained sovereignty. Blue represents the country's lakes and sky as well as loyalty and the ancient longing for freedom; black stands for the dark soil of the homeland and the hardships endured; and white evokes birch bark, snow, and the striving toward enlightenment and hope.
