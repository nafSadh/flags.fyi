# Flag of Tanzania

Tanzania's flag features a gold-edged black diagonal band running from the lower hoist corner, dividing the field into a green upper triangle and blue lower triangle. Adopted in 1964 following the union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar.

Green represents the agricultural lands, black symbolizes the people, blue represents the Indian Ocean and waterways, and yellow represents mineral wealth. The diagonal arrangement ensures distinctiveness among world flags. The symbolism comprehensively represents the nation's natural resources, geography, and people.
