# Flag of Tristan da Cunha

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms of Tristan da Cunha. The arms feature Tristan longboats, a Tristan rock lobster, and a naval crown, with the motto 'Our faith is our strength'.

Tristan da Cunha uses the Union Jack as a British Overseas Territory. One of the most remote inhabited places in the world. Smallest population of any British territory.
