Flag of the Republic of Afghanistan

Description

  • The flag of the Republic of Afghanistan (1973–1978) used the same black-red-green vertical tricolour with a modified emblem omitting the royal crown
  • After Mohammed Daoud Khan's republican coup in 1973, the monarchy was abolished and the flag's emblem was updated to reflect the new republic
  • This flag was short-lived, replaced after the Saur Revolution in 1978
  • Construction: three equal vertical bands of black (hoist), red, and green. The eagle-based republican emblem sits centred. Ratio 2:3

Trivia

  • Adopted after Mohammed Daoud Khan's 1973 coup that abolished the 240-year monarchy
  • Lasted only five years before the communist Saur Revolution replaced it in 1978
  • The republican emblem omitted the royal crown, symbolising the end of monarchy
