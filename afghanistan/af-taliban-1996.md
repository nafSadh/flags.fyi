# Flag of the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan (1996)

The flag used by the Taliban during their first rule (1996–2001) was a plain white field bearing the Shahada (Islamic declaration of faith) in black Arabic script. This stark, minimalist design signalled the Taliban's austere interpretation of Islamic governance.

One of only two flags in the world that is a plain colour with only text (the other being Libya's old green flag). The Shahada (Islamic declaration of faith) is the world's most common phrase on national flags. Used by the Taliban in both their 1996–2001 and 2021–present periods of rule.
