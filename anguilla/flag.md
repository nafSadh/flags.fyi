# Flag of Anguilla

A Blue Ensign defaced with the coat of arms of Anguilla, featuring three orange dolphins. The dolphins symbolise endurance, unity, and strength. The badge of Anguilla (three dolphins) is centred in the fly half.

The dolphins represent grace and swiftness; the orange, blue, green, and white show Caribbean colours. Adopted in 1990 after Anguilla separated from St. Kitts and Nevis. The dolphins are stylized versions of spotted dolphins found in Caribbean waters around Anguilla.
