# Flag of the Governor of Anguilla

The Governor's flag of Anguilla is a Union Flag (Union Jack) defaced with the Anguillan coat of arms on a white disc in the centre. It represents the Governor as the representative of the British Crown in Anguilla, a British Overseas Territory.

Official flag used by the Governor of Anguilla. Represents executive authority and British colonial ties. Uses heraldic symbols reflecting Anguilla's governance structure.
