# Dolphin Flag of Anguilla

An unofficial local flag of Anguilla featuring three orange dolphins arranged in a circle on a white field above a turquoise sea. The three dolphins symbolise friendship, wisdom, and strength — the three core values of the Anguillan people.

Alternative Anguilla flag design emphasizing the dolphin symbolism. Dolphins are central to Anguillian maritime culture and identity. This flag variant celebrates the island's marine biodiversity.
