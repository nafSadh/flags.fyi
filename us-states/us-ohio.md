# Flag of Ohio (Burgee)

Ohio's flag is the only non-rectangular US state flag — a burgee (swallowtail) pennant. The red-white-blue design features a circle and 17 stars. A blue triangle based on the hoist extends into a red and white striped field (five stripes).

The pennant design (not rectangular) is unique among US state flags. The colours represent Ohio's history and natural resources. Ohio adopted this flag in 1902.
