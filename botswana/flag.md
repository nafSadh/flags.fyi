# Flag of Botswana

Botswana's flag is a light blue field crossed by a horizontal black stripe bordered with thin white bands. Adopted on 30 September 1966, the day the country became independent from the United Kingdom, the design breaks from the Pan-African palette common elsewhere on the continent.

The light blue represents water and sky, a vital resource in the largely arid Kalahari, and echoes the national motto Pula, meaning rain. The black stripe framed by white evokes the zebra, the national animal, and stands for racial harmony between the nation's peoples. The choice of colours was also a deliberate contrast with the red, white and blue of neighbouring apartheid-era South Africa.
