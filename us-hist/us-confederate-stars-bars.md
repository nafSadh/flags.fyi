# First Flag of the Confederate States

The 'Stars and Bars' was the first national flag of the Confederate States of America (1861–1863), featuring a blue canton with a circle of white stars on three stripes of red, white, and red. Often confused with the more famous Confederate Battle Flag, the Stars and Bars was replaced because it too closely resembled the US flag on the battlefield, causing confusion.

The original Confederate flag featured stars and bars. Used by the Confederate States of America (1861–1865). Later appropriated by segregationists and hate groups.
