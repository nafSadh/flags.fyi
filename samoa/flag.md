# Flag of Samoa

Samoa's flag was adopted on 24 February 1949, when the country was still the Trust Territory of Western Samoa under New Zealand administration. It has been kept unchanged through independence in 1962 and the 1997 constitutional change that shortened the country's name from Western Samoa to Samoa.

The design features a red field with a blue rectangle in the upper hoist quadrant bearing the five white stars of the Southern Cross constellation. Red represents courage, white stands for purity, and the blue canton symbolises freedom. The original 1948 version carried only four stars; a fifth smaller star, Epsilon Crucis, was added in 1949 to match the full Southern Cross and give the flag its distinctive Polynesian identity shared with several other Oceania nations.
