# Flag of the Republic of Venice

The Most Serene Republic of Venice (697–1797) flew the Lion of Saint Mark on a red field, one of the most recognisable maritime flags in history. The lion is in passant position with its front paw resting on the book.

Venice was a powerful medieval and Renaissance city-state and republic. The winged lion represented Venetian power and maritime dominance. One of history's greatest trading powers.
