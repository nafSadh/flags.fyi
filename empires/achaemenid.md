# Standard of the Achaemenid Empire

The Achaemenid (Persian) Empire (550–330 BC) used the Derafsh Kaviani, a jewelled royal standard. It was the precursor to the Sassanid standard. Achaemenid standards were military vexilloids rather than rectangular flags.

The Achaemenid Empire (550–330 BCE) was one of history's largest empires. Created by Cyrus the Great and conquered territories from Egypt to India. Known for advanced administration and the Royal Road trade network.
