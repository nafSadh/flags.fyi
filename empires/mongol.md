# Banner of the Mongol Empire

The Mongol Empire (1206–1368) used the white sulde (spirit banner) with nine horse-hair tails, a sacred symbol representing the spirit of Genghis Khan. Mongol standards were often mounted on a pole with horsehair tassels (tugs).

The Mongol Empire (1206–1368) was one of history's largest empires. The flag represented the vast territorial extent of Mongol rule. Conquered territories from Europe to the Pacific.
