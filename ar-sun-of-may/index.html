Flag of Argentina with Sun of May

Description

  • The Sun of May (Sol de Mayo) was added to the Argentine flag in 1818, symbolising the May Revolution of 1810 and the Inca sun god Inti
  • Construction: the Sun of May emblem alone — a 32-ray alternating straight-and-wavy sun with a human face, rendered in gold/yellow

Trivia

  • The Sun of May (Inti in Quechua) features prominently in Argentina's heraldry
  • The sun has a human face with alternating straight and wavy rays
  • The symbol comes from Incan sun worship traditions

Flag of Argentina with Sun of May

The Sun of May (Sol de Mayo) was added to the Argentine flag in 1818, symbolising the May Revolution of 1810 and the Inca sun god Inti.

The Sun of May (Inti in Quechua) features prominently in Argentina's heraldry. The sun has a human face with alternating straight and wavy rays. The symbol comes from Incan sun worship traditions.
