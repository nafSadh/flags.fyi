Hashemite Kingdom of Iraq

Description

  • The flag of the Hashemite Kingdom of Iraq (1924–1958) continued the Pan-Arab colour scheme with black, white, and green horizontal bands and a red trapezoid at the hoist
  • This flag represented the Hashemite monarchy established by King Faisal I
  • It was abandoned after the July 1958 revolution that overthrew the monarchy
  • Construction: three horizontal stripes of black (top), white, and green with a red trapezoid at the hoist containing two white seven-pointed stars
  • Ratio approximately 1:2

Trivia

  • The Hashemite kingdom of Iraq (1921–1958) was established by the British mandate
  • Represented the Hashemite dynasty's rule in Iraq
  • Overthrown in the 1958 Iraqi Revolution

Hashemite Kingdom of Iraq

The flag of the Hashemite Kingdom of Iraq (1924–1958) continued the Pan-Arab colour scheme with black, white, and green horizontal bands and a red trapezoid at the hoist. This flag represented the Hashemite monarchy established by King Faisal I.

The Hashemite kingdom of Iraq (1921–1958) was established by the British mandate. Represented the Hashemite dynasty's rule in Iraq. Overthrown in the 1958 Iraqi Revolution.
