# Flag of the Pitcairn Islands

The flag of the Pitcairn Islands is a Blue Ensign with the Union Jack in the canton and the Pitcairn coat of arms in the fly — a shield showing the Bible and anchor from HMS Bounty on a green triangle. Adopted on 2 April 1984, the coat of arms represents the islands' unique history: the Bounty mutineers and their Tahitian companions who founded the settlement in 1790.

Pitcairn Islands use the Union Jack as they are a British Overseas Territory. One of the least populated territories in the world. The inhabitants are primarily descendants of HMS Bounty mutineers.
