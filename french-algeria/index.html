Flag of French Algeria

French Algeria (1830–1962) was considered an integral part of France and flew the French tricolour as its official flag.

French Algeria was a French colony from 1830 to 1962. The Union Jack represented French colonial rule. Algeria's independence war (1954–1962) was particularly brutal.
