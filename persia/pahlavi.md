# Flag of the Pahlavi Dynasty (Imperial Iran)

The Pahlavi Dynasty (1925–1979) used the green-white-red tricolour with the lion-and-sun emblem. This was replaced after the 1979 Islamic Revolution.

The Pahlavi dynasty (1925–1979) ruled Iran under Reza Shah and Mohammad Reza Shah. The lion and sun symbolised Persian power and mythology. Overthrown in the Iranian Revolution of 1979.
