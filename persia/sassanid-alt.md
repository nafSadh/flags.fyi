# Derafsh Kaviani (alternate)

An alternative representation of the Sassanid Empire's banner, typically featuring the Derafsh Kaviani — a legendary royal standard with streamers and precious stones. The Sassanid Empire (224–651 CE) was the last pre-Islamic Persian empire.

Alternative Sassanid flag design with different symbolism. Represents variations in royal banners and regalia. Shows the evolution of Sassanid heraldic traditions.
