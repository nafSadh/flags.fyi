# Flag of Madagascar

Madagascar's flag consists of a vertical white band at the hoist and two horizontal bands of red above green on the fly. Adopted on 14 October 1958, ahead of full independence from France in 1960, the design blends the colours of the former Merina kingdom with green to represent the Hova peasant class. The combination of red and white originally flew over the Kingdom of Madagascar that unified much of the island in the nineteenth century.

Symbolically, white stands for purity, red for sovereignty, and green for hope and the island's coastal peoples. The flag's unusual layout, with a hoist-side vertical stripe rather than the typical canton or horizontal tricolour, distinguishes it from most post-colonial African flags and has remained unchanged through successive republics.
