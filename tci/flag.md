# Flag of the Turks and Caicos Islands

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms of the Turks and Caicos Islands featuring a conch shell, spiny lobster, and Turk's-head cactus. These elements represent the islands' marine life and distinctive flora.

The Turks and Caicos Islands use the Union Jack. Reflecting their status as a British Overseas Territory. The islands are known for beaches and tourism.
