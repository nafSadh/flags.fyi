# Flag of the Provisional Republic of Brazil

The Provisional Flag of the Republic of Brazil (15–19 November 1889) was used for four days after the overthrow of the Empire. It was inspired by the United States flag, with 13 green and yellow horizontal stripes and a blue canton with white stars.

Brazil's provisional flag was used during the transition from empire to republic. Represents the period of political transformation in the 1890s. Shows the evolution of Brazilian national symbols.
