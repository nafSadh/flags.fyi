# Flag of the Cayman Islands

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms of the Cayman Islands featuring a pineapple, turtle, and three stars. The three stars represent the three islands: Grand Cayman, Cayman Brac, and Little Cayman. The coat of arms (shield with wavy bands, turtle, pineapple) appears in the fly half.

The Cayman Islands are a British Overseas Territory in the Caribbean. The flag features the Union Jack and the territory's coat of arms. Known for being a major international financial center.
