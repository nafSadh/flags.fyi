# Flag of the Republic of Yemen

Three equal horizontal stripes of red (top), white, and black (bottom) — the Arab Liberation flag colours. Yemen adopted this tricolour upon the unification of North Yemen and South Yemen on 22 May 1990. The design removes the star (North) and triangle (South) from the two predecessor flags, symbolising unity.

The red-white-black tricolour are Pan-Arab colours. The flag was unified in 1990 after the merger of North and South Yemen. Yemen's flag emphasizes the nation's Arab identity.
