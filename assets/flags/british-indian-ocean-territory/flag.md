# Flag of the British Indian Ocean Territory

A Blue Ensign defaced with the territory's coat of arms — a palm tree atop wavy blue-and-white stripes with a crown above, and the Union Flag in the canton. Adopted on 8 November 1990 for the British Indian Ocean Territory, which includes Diego Garcia.

This British territory consists of the Chagos Archipelago in the Indian Ocean. The Union Jack appears in the flag. Territorial claims by Mauritius over this region remain disputed.
