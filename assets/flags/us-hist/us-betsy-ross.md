# Betsy Ross Flag (13 Stars)

The Betsy Ross flag features 13 red and white alternating stripes with a circle of 13 white five-pointed stars on a blue canton — representing the original 13 colonies. Tradition credits Betsy Ross with sewing the first American flag in 1776 at George Washington's request, though this story is debated by historians.

The Betsy Ross flag is a popular symbol of early American independence. Allegedly sewn by Betsy Ross, though historical evidence is unclear. Featured 13 stars in a circle representing the 13 original states.
