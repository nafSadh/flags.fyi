# Flag of Chad

Three equal vertical bands of blue (hoist side), yellow, and red. Adopted on 6 November 1959, the design is a compromise between the French Tricolore and the Pan-African colours. The flag is nearly identical to Romania's, differing only in a slightly darker shade of blue.

The blue, gold, and red stripes represent hope, prosperity, and blood shed for independence. The colours are Pan-African, adopted like many African nations post-1950s. Chad and Romania's flags are nearly identical (the exact same colours and proportions), causing rare diplomatic confusion.
