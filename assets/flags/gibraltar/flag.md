# Flag of Gibraltar

A white field over a red field in a 2:1 ratio, with a three-towered red castle and a golden key hanging from the gate. One of the oldest flags in the world, granted by Royal Warrant of Queen Isabella I of Castile in 1502.

Gibraltar's flag reflects the contested status between Britain and Spain. The white and red castle (fortress) represents the Rock of Gibraltar. Gibraltar uses the Union Jack as its official flag but has a unique local flag for unofficial use.
