# Flag of the Republic of Korea (Taegeukgi)

A white field with a red-and-blue taegeuk (yin-yang symbol) at the centre, surrounded by four black trigrams from the I Ching. The taegeuk represents the balance of cosmic forces. The four trigrams — geon (heaven), gon (earth), gam (water), and ri (fire) — symbolise the principles of movement and harmony.

The trigrams are from the I Ching (Book of Changes); the taegeuk (circle) represents balance between cosmic forces. The red and blue are complementary colours representing the harmony of Yin and Yang. South Korea adopted this flag in 1945; the design is based on ancient Korean symbolism.
