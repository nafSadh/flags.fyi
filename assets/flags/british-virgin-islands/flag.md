# Flag of the British Virgin Islands

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms featuring Saint Ursula holding a lamp, surrounded by eleven smaller lamps. The arms refer to the territory's namesake, the legend of Saint Ursula and the 11,000 Virgins. The territorial arms (Saint Ursula with lamps) appear in the fly half.

The Virgin Islands are British Overseas Territories in the Caribbean. The flag features the Union Jack and the territory's coat of arms. Known for financial services and tourism industries.
