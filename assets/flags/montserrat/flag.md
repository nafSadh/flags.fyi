# Flag of Montserrat

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms of Montserrat in the fly. The arms depict a woman in green, Erin, embracing a black cross and golden harp, reflecting the island's Irish heritage.

Montserrat uses the Union Jack as it is a British Overseas Territory. The island's flag includes the harp (Ireland's symbol) and cross-symbols. Montserrat is partially uninhabitable due to volcanic activity from the Soufrière Hills volcano.
