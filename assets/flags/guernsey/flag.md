# Flag of Guernsey

A white field with a red St George's Cross and a golden cross of William the Conqueror within it. The gold cross was added in 1985 to distinguish it from the flag of England. Inside the cross, a gold Cross of William the Conqueror is centred.

Guernsey uses a modified Union Jack with a cross representing St. George. Guernsey is a Crown dependency with its own government but under British sovereignty. The island has its own local flag separate from the official Union Jack.
