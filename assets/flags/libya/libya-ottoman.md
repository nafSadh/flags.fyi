# Flag of Ottoman Libya

The coastal region of what is today **Libya** was ruled by the Ottoman Empire. - from 1551 to 1864, as the Eyalet of Tripolitania (Ottoman Turkish: ایالت طرابلس غرب‎ Eyālet-i Trâblus Gârb). - from 1864 to 1912, as the Vilayet of Tripolitania (Ottoman Turkish: ولايت طرابلس غرب‎ Vilâyet-i Trâblus Gârb).

Ottoman Libya used the standard Ottoman red flag with crescent and star. The Ottomans controlled Libya for nearly 400 years (1551–1911) before losing it to Italy.
