# Flag of the British Antarctic Territory

A British White Ensign (later Blue Ensign) defaced with the territory's coat of arms, which features a flaming torch on a shield supported by the ship HMS Endurance and a lion. Adopted on 21 March 1963 when the territory was separated from the Falkland Islands Dependencies.

This Antarctic territory includes several islands used primarily for scientific research. The Union Jack reflects British sovereignty over the region. Few permanent residents; used mainly for scientific bases.
