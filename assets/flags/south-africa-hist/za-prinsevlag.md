# Flag of South Africa (1928–1994)

The Prinsevlag (Prince's Flag) of South Africa was an orange, white, and blue horizontal tricolour — derived from the Dutch Prince's Flag — flown as part of the apartheid-era national flag (1928–1994). The South African flag of 1928 incorporated a central panel showing the Prinsevlag alongside the Union Jack and the flags of the two Boer republics.

The Prinsevlag (Prince's Flag) is an alternative South African flag design. Represents historical Dutch/Afrikaner heritage. Used by some cultural and historical societies.
