# Flag of Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia's flag features a green background with white Arabic text and a sword. The inscription displays the Shahada: "There is no deity but God; Muhammad is the Messenger of God." Green symbolizes Islam, believed to be the Prophet Muhammad's favourite colour.

The white sword represents justice and the defence of faith. In 1921, Abdulaziz Al-Saud added a sword to the traditional Shahada banner. The flag is manufactured identically on both sides to ensure the Arabic text reads correctly from either direction. Because it bears the Shahada, the flag is never flown at half-mast.
