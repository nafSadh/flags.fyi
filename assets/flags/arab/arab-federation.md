# Arab Federation of Iraq and Jordan

The Arab Federation of Iraq and Jordan was a short-lived country that was formed in 1958. from the union of Iraq and Jordan. Although the name implies a federal structure, it was. _de facto_ a confederation.

The Arab Federation (1958) briefly united the Hashemite kingdoms of Iraq and Jordan. The flag represented Pan-Arab unity and Hashemite dynastic power. The federation lasted only six months before the Iraqi Revolution of 1958.
