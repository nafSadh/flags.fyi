# Flag of Alderney

A white field with a red St George's Cross bearing the coat of arms of Alderney at its centre. The arms feature a golden lion on a green field. Adopted in 1993. The coat of arms is placed at the intersection of the cross.

Alderney is one of the Channel Islands with its own distinct flag and governance. The island has a population of around 2,000 people. Alderney maintains its own culture and traditions distinct from mainland Britain.
