# Progress Pride Flag

The six-stripe rainbow flag with an added chevron of five arrow-shaped stripes (white, pink, light blue, brown, and black) extending from the hoist. Designed by Daniel Quasar in 2018 to incorporate the trans flag colours and stripes representing people of colour and those living with or lost to AIDS.

The chevron pointing right represents forward progress and was designed by Daniel Quasar in 2018. The design incorporates the trans pride flag colours and adds emphasis on marginalized communities. The flag emphasizes that the LGBTQ+ movement continues to progress toward equality.
