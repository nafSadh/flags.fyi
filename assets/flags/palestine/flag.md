# Flag of Palestine

Three horizontal stripes of black, white, and green with a red triangle at the hoist — based on the Pan-Arab colours first used during the 1916 Arab Revolt. The four colours represent four great Arab dynasties: the Abbasids (black), Umayyads (white), Fatimids (green), and Hashemites (red).

The flag is nearly identical to the Jordanian flag but without the Hashemite star. The colours are Pan-Arab colours representing Arab solidarity and Palestinian identity. The flag represents the Palestinian national authority and independence aspirations.
