# Flag of the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic

The flag of the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic was a red field bearing the Cyrillic letters 'РСФСР' (or, in later versions, the hammer and sickle with a blue vertical stripe at the hoist). The RSFSR was the largest and most populous republic of the USSR (1917–1991).

The Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (RSFSR) was the largest Soviet republic. The hammer and sickle represented communist ideology. Became the Russian Federation after the Soviet Union collapsed.
