# Flag of Andalusia

The flag of Andalusia features two equal horizontal stripes of green (top) and white. The green represents hope and the open fields; the white represents the whitewashed towns (pueblos blancos) and peace. Designed by Blas Infante, the 'father of Andalusian nationalism', in 1918, it draws on the colours of the Umayyad Caliphate and the Almohad dynasty — reflecting the Islamic heritage of Al-Andalus.

Andalusia is a region in southern Spain with Moorish heritage. The flag represents Andalusian identity and autonomy within Spain. The green represents Islamic heritage; white represents purity.
