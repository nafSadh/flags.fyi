# Flag of Sark

The flag of Sark features a white field with a red Cross of St George (the English cross), defaced by two golden Norman lions passant in the upper-hoist canton. Adopted on 16 November 1938, the flag reflects Sark's status as a Crown dependency within the Bailiwick of Guernsey.

Sark is a British Crown dependency in the Channel Islands. Has its own distinct flag and feudal governance system. One of the last feudal territories in Europe.
