# Flag of Siam (White Elephant)

The Kingdom of Siam (1855–1916) used a red field with a white elephant in the centre — one of the most distinctive historical flags of Southeast Asia. Used as the national flag of Siam before the adoption of the current tricolour in 1917.

Thailand (formerly Siam) used the white elephant symbol historically. The elephant represented royal power and Buddhist sacred traditions. Still used in Thai royal symbolism and heraldry.
