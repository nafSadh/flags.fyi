# Juneteenth Flag

A flag commemorating the emancipation of enslaved African Americans, featuring a bursting star on a curved horizon. Designed by Lisa Jeanne Graf in 1997. The star represents a new beginning and the burst symbolises new freedoms.

Juneteenth commemorates the announcement of enslaved people's freedom in Galveston, Texas (1865). The flag represents African American freedom and liberation. Became a federal holiday in the United States in 2021.
