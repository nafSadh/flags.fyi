# Flag of the Rashidun Caliphate

The Rashidun Caliphate (632–661) was the first of the four major Islamic caliphates. Its banner was plain white, symbolising purity and the legacy of the Prophet.

The Rashidun Caliphate (632–661) was the first Islamic state and empire. Represented the beginning of Islamic expansionist period. Established the foundation for Islamic civilisation.
