# War Flag of the Imperial Japanese Army

The flag of the Imperial Japanese Army was a red disc with 16 red rays extending to the edges on a white field — the Kyokujitsu-ki (Rising Sun Flag). Used from 1870 until the end of World War II in 1945, the flag remains controversial in East Asia due to its association with Japanese militarism and wartime atrocities.
