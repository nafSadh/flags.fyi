# Flag of Autonomous Island of Mohéli

The flag of Mohéli, the smallest of the main Comoros islands, has varied over the years but typically features the island's distinct identity within the Comoros union. Mohéli has historically been the quietest of the four Comoros islands, with less secessionist activity than Anjouan.

Mohéli is the smallest of the four main Comoros islands and has the country's only marine national park. The island is known as a major nesting site for green sea turtles.
