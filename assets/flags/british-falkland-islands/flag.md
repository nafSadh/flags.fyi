# Flag of the Falkland Islands

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms of the Falkland Islands in the fly. The arms feature a ram above a sailing ship (Desire), representing the islands' sheep farming and maritime heritage.

The Falkland Islands are claimed by both Britain and Argentina. The flag shows the Union Jack and the Falkland Islands coat of arms. The 1982 Falkland Islands War was fought over territorial control.
