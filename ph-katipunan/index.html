Flag of the Katipunan

Description

  • The flag of the Katipunan (Kataas-taasan, Kagalang-galangang Katipunan ng mga Anak ng Bayan) was a red field with a white sun — the banner of the revolutionary society that launched the Philippine Revolution in 1896
  • Founded by Andrés Bonifacio, the Katipunan used several flag variants
  • The most common featured a white mythological sun (representing liberty) on a red field (representing the blood of revolution)
  • Construction: a red field with a white sun device centred. Ratio approximately 2:3

Trivia

  • The Katipunan was the revolutionary group that fought Philippine independence
  • The flag represented the independence movement against Spanish rule
  • Led the Philippine Revolution of 1896
