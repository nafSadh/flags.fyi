# Flag of Iceland

Iceland's flag features a blue field bearing an off-centre red Nordic cross that is itself bordered in white, with the horizontal arm of the cross extending to the right. The design places Iceland squarely within the family of Nordic cross flags shared by Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland, and the Faroe Islands, reflecting shared Scandinavian heritage and historical ties.

The tricolour combination evokes the Icelandic landscape: blue for the Atlantic Ocean and the mountains, white for the glaciers and snow that cover much of the island, and red for the volcanic fire that continually reshapes the land. First hoisted in 1915 and adopted as a civil flag in 1918 under Danish rule, the current form was confirmed as the national flag on 17 June 1944 when Iceland became a republic.
