# Flag of Abkhazia

Seven alternating green and white horizontal stripes (starting with green) with a red canton bearing a white open right hand and seven white five-pointed stars arranged in an arc. The seven stripes and seven stars represent the seven historic districts of Abkhazia.

Abkhazia is a partially recognized territory with contested sovereignty. Recognized by Russia but not by most of the international community. Located on the Black Sea coast between Russia and Georgia.
