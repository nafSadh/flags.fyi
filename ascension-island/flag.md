# Flag of Ascension Island

The unofficial flag of Ascension Island is a Blue Ensign with the territory's coat of arms in the fly. The arms feature two turtles and a mountain representing Green Mountain. Ascension Island, a volcanic island in the South Atlantic, is a dependency of Saint Helena.

Ascension Island is a remote British territory in the Atlantic Ocean. The island has a tiny population of around 800 people. Home to an important military base and BBC relay station.
