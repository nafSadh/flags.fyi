# Flag of the United Arab Emirates

A vertical red stripe at the hoist with three equal horizontal stripes of green (top), white, and black (bottom). The four colours are the Pan-Arab colours, each representing a different Arab dynasty: green for the Fatimids, white for the Umayyads, black for the Abbasids, and red for the Hashemites.

The red band represents courage and sacrifice; the green represents hope and prosperity. The black represents the power and determination of the people. The UAE adopted this flag in 1971 upon its formation from seven emirates.
