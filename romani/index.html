Flag of the Romani People

Description

  • The Romani flag (adopted 1971) features blue (sky/heaven) and green (earth/nature) halves with a red chakra wheel in the centre
  • Construction: two equal horizontal bands of blue (top) and green (bottom), with a red sixteen-spoke chakra (wheel) centred on the dividing line
  • The wheel represents the Romani travelling tradition

Trivia

  • The Romani people flag represents the Romani ethnic group across Europe
  • The colours represent the sun (yellow) and homeland (blue)
  • The wheel represents Romani culture and nomadic heritage

Flag of the Romani People

The Romani flag (adopted 1971) features blue (sky/heaven) and green (earth/nature) halves with a red chakra wheel in the centre. The wheel represents the Romani travelling tradition.

The Romani people flag represents the Romani ethnic group across Europe. The colours represent the sun (yellow) and homeland (blue). The wheel represents Romani culture and nomadic heritage.
