# Flag of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea

A broad red central stripe bordered by thin white and then blue stripes, with a white disc containing a red five-pointed star on the hoist side. The red star on the white disc symbolises the revolutionary traditions and the guiding ideology of Juche, established by Kim Il-sung.

North Korea's flag was designed to deliberately reject the traditional Korean taegeuk symbol. The white circle represents purity; the red represents revolution and socialism. North Korea adopted this flag in 1948 to emphasize its communist ideology separate from the South.
