# Flag of Mauritania

Mauritania's flag features a green field with a gold crescent and five-pointed star, bordered by red stripes at the top and bottom. The green and gold design dates to independence in 1959, while the red stripes were added on 15 August 2017 following a constitutional referendum.

The green field represents Islam and the hope of a bright future, the gold crescent and star are pan-Islamic symbols, and the red stripes honour those who died defending the nation. A distinctive feature is the crescent's upward-facing horns, setting it apart from most other flags that use the crescent motif.
