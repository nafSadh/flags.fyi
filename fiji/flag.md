# Flag of Fiji

Fiji's flag was adopted on 10 October 1970, marking independence from the United Kingdom. The design reflects both Commonwealth heritage and Pacific Island identity. The light blue field symbolizes the Pacific Ocean's central importance to Fijian life, culture, and economy.

The Union Jack in the upper left corner acknowledges Fiji's historical ties to Britain and the Commonwealth. The shield from Fiji's coat of arms features a heraldic lion holding a cocoa pod and images of sugarcane, coconut palms, and bananas representing agricultural heritage. Though proposals to change the flag were announced in 2013, they were abandoned in 2016.
