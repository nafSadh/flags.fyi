# Flag of Palau

Palau adopted its flag on 1 January 1981, the day the country achieved internal self-government under the Compact of Free Association. The design, selected from a public competition, shows a large yellow disc slightly offset toward the hoist on a light blue field.

The light blue represents the Pacific Ocean and Palau's transition from foreign domination — first Spanish, then German, Japanese, and finally American trusteeship — to self-government. The yellow disc depicts not the sun but the full moon, considered in Palauan tradition the optimal time for fishing, planting, harvesting, and community celebrations. Although the colour scheme resembles Japan's flag, the deliberate off-centre placement and moon symbolism set Palau's ensign firmly within its own Micronesian cultural tradition.
