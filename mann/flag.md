# Flag of the Isle of Man

A red field with the triskelion (three armoured legs) at the centre. The triskelion has been a symbol of the Isle of Man for centuries. The motto 'Quocunque Jeceris Stabit' (Whichever way you throw me, I will stand) accompanies the emblem.

The Isle of Man is a British Crown dependency with its own distinct flag. The three-legged symbol (triskele) represents endurance and resilience. The legs rotate clockwise and appear to be in motion.
