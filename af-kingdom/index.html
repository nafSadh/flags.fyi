Flag of the Kingdom of Afghanistan

The flag of the Kingdom of Afghanistan (1929–1973) was a black, red, and green vertical tricolour with a white national emblem centred. This design continued the tradition of the Afghan tricolour but with royal emblems reflecting the monarchy under King Mohammed Zahir Shah.

Afghanistan's royal emblem on this flag reflected King Mohammed Zahir Shah's modernization efforts. The kingdom lasted from 1929–1973, during Afghanistan's most stable modern period. This design bridged traditional Afghan symbols with Western heraldic conventions.
