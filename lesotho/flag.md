# Flag of Lesotho

Lesotho's flag has three horizontal bands of blue, white and green with a black mokorotlo, the traditional Basotho straw hat, centred on the white band. The colours are read as blue for the rain that gives the mountain kingdom its water, white for peace, and green for prosperity. The mokorotlo stands for the Basotho people and their indigenous culture.

Adopted on 4 October 2006, the flag was unveiled on the 40th anniversary of independence from the United Kingdom. It replaced a 1987 design featuring a Basotho shield, assegai and knobkerrie, symbols associated with the military government of the time. The peaceful new flag deliberately set aside all martial imagery in favour of a unifying cultural icon.
