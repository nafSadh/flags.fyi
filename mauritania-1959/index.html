Former flag of Mauritania (1959–2017)

Description

  • The original flag of Mauritania (1959–2017) was a green field with a gold crescent and star — without the red top and bottom stripes that were added in 2017
  • This design was adopted upon independence from France on 28 November 1960 and remained unchanged for nearly six decades until a constitutional referendum on 5 August 2017 added the red stripes
  • Construction: a green field with a gold crescent (horns upward) and five-pointed star centred. Ratio 2:3

Trivia

  • Mauritania's original flag from 1959 upon independence from France
  • Represented the nation's Islamic identity and Arab heritage
  • Evolved with the addition of the Shahada inscription later

Former flag of Mauritania (1959–2017)

The original flag of Mauritania (1959–2017) was a green field with a gold crescent and star — without the red top and bottom stripes that were added in 2017. This design was adopted upon independence from France on 28 November 1960 and remained unchanged for nearly six decades until a constitutional referendum on 5 August 2017 added the red stripes.

Mauritania's original flag from 1959 upon independence from France. Represented the nation's Islamic identity and Arab heritage. Evolved with the addition of the Shahada inscription later.
