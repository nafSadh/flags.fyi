# Emblems of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement

The Red Cross (1863) and Red Crescent (1876) emblems are protected symbols of humanitarian neutrality. The crystal (2005) serves as a third emblem. The cross arms are of equal length and width. The Red Crescent variant uses a red crescent on white.

The Red Cross (International Committee of the Red Cross) is a humanitarian organisation. The flag represents neutrality, impartiality, and humanitarian protection. Founded in 1863 to provide relief during conflicts.
