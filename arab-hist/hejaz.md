# Flag of the Kingdom of Hejaz

The Kingdom of Hejaz (1916–1925) flag was the original Arab Revolt flag — the direct ancestor of the Pan-Arab flag and the flags of Jordan, Palestine, and others.

The Kingdom of Hejaz (1916–1925) was an early Arab state in the Arabian Peninsula. Briefly independent during the Arab Revolt against Ottoman rule. Conquered by Saudi Arabia and incorporated into the modern kingdom.
