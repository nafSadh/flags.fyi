# First Flag of Bangladesh (1971)

The flag used during the Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971 was a green field with a red disc, inside which was a golden map of what was then East Pakistan. Designed by student leader Quamrul Hasan, this version was first hoisted on 2 March 1971 at Dhaka University.

During the 1971 independence war, the flag included a map of Bangladesh. This variant symbolised the geographic identity of the emerging nation. The map variant was used briefly during the war of independence.
