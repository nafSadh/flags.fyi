# White Ensign of the Royal Navy

The White Ensign (St George's Ensign) is the ensign of the Royal Navy, featuring a white field with the red Cross of St George and the Union Jack in the canton. It has been used by the Royal Navy since 1864 and is one of the most recognisable naval ensigns in the world.

The White Ensign is used by the British Royal Navy. Features the Union Jack and the cross of St. George. One of the oldest naval flags still in use.
